Everlit Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Taste Games (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in Everlit (the “App”), a habit tracker for iPhone. If you have questions, contact us at tastegames13@gmail.com.

The short version

Everlit works entirely on your device. Your habits, your notes and your photos never leave it. The App has no accounts, no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no tracking, and we do not operate any server that receives data from the App. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal information.

Your habits

The habits you create, the days you mark, and the values and notes you add are kept in a file on your device, in the storage the App shares with its own widgets. They are not uploaded, not sent to us, and not shared with anyone. This data is removed when you delete the App.

You can export your data as a file from the App’s settings. The file goes only where you choose to send it.

Photos and the camera

If you add a photo to a day, the App uses the camera with your permission, or a picture you pick from your library. The photo is stored on your device with the rest of the App’s data and is not uploaded. You can withdraw the camera permission at any time in the Settings app, under Privacy & Security, Camera.

Reminders, widgets, Siri and Shortcuts

Reminders are notifications that the App schedules on your device; no server is involved. Widgets read the App’s data on your device. When you use Siri or the Shortcuts app with Everlit, your request is handled by your device and by Apple, as described in the Apple Privacy Policy; the App itself sends nothing.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information through the App. Everlit does not ask for your name, email address, or any other contact details, and it does not use advertising identifiers. Your settings, and simple counters of which features have been used, are stored only on your device and are never sent to us.

Purchases

Everlit Pro subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details, billing address, or Apple Account information. The App checks on your device, through Apple’s systems, whether a purchase is active. Apple’s handling of your information is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

Information Apple may share with us

If you have chosen on your device to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide us with aggregated statistics and crash reports about the App. This information does not identify you. Apple also gives us aggregated sales reports.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use this only to reply to you and to resolve your request, we keep it only for as long as that takes or as the law requires, and we do not sell or share it.

Children’s privacy

Everlit is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children or from anyone else through the App. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, please contact us so that we can delete it.

Data retention and security

Because the App keeps its data on your device, that data is protected by the security of your device and stays there until you delete the App. We hold no copy of it.

International data processing

The App does not send your data anywhere, so it is not transferred to other countries by us. Email you send us is handled by our email provider and may be processed in countries other than the one where you live.

Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, including the General Data Protection Regulation in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, and privacy laws of states in the United States such as California, you may have rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, and portability, and the right not to be discriminated against for using them. Since the App does not collect personal information, we generally hold nothing about you. You remain in control of the data on your device: you can withdraw the camera permission, export your data, or delete the App at any time.

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for targeted advertising. For correspondence you have sent us, you can ask us for a copy or to delete it by emailing tastegames13@gmail.com. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the App or our practices. Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

For questions about this policy or Everlit, contact Taste Games at tastegames13@gmail.com.
