Limpid Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Taste Games (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in Limpid (the “App”), a photo library cleaner for iPhone and iPad. If you have questions, contact us at tastegames13@gmail.com.

The short version

Limpid works entirely on your device. Your photos and videos never leave it. The App has no accounts, no advertising, no third-party analytics, and no tracking, and we do not operate any server that receives data from the App. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal information.

Photos and videos

With your permission, Limpid reads the photo library on your device to count your photos and videos, measure their sizes, and find duplicates, similar shots, blurry photos, screenshots, and large videos. All of this analysis happens on your device.

Your photos and videos, the information stored with them (such as dates and locations), and the results of the analysis are not uploaded, not sent to us, and not shared with anyone. The App stores the results of its analysis on your device so that later scans are faster; this data is removed when you delete the App.

You decide how much of your library the App can see. You can change or withdraw this permission at any time in the Settings app, under Privacy & Security, Photos.

Deleting and compressing

Limpid never deletes anything on its own. Items you choose to let go are removed only after you confirm in the confirmation shown by your device, and they are moved to the Recently Deleted album of the Photos app, where your device keeps them for a limited time before removing them permanently.

When you compress a video, the compression is done on your device and the new copy is saved to your own photo library.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information through the App. Limpid does not ask for your name, email address, or any other contact details, and it does not use advertising identifiers. Your settings and your statistics, such as how much space you have freed, are stored only on your device, including in the storage the App shares with its own widget.

Purchases

Limpid Pro subscriptions and purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details, billing address, or Apple Account information. The App checks on your device, through Apple’s systems, whether a purchase is active. Apple’s handling of your information is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

Information Apple may share with us

If you have chosen on your device to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide us with aggregated statistics and crash reports about the App. This information does not identify you. Apple also gives us aggregated sales reports.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use this only to reply to you and to resolve your request, we keep it only for as long as that takes or as the law requires, and we do not sell or share it.

Children’s privacy

Limpid is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children or from anyone else through the App. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, please contact us so that we can delete it.

Data retention and security

Because the App keeps its data on your device, that data is protected by the security of your device and stays there until you delete the App. We hold no copy of it.

International data processing

The App does not send your data anywhere, so it is not transferred to other countries by us. Email you send us is handled by our email provider and may be processed in countries other than the one where you live.

Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, including the General Data Protection Regulation in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, and privacy laws of states in the United States such as California, you may have rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, and portability, and the right not to be discriminated against for using them. Since the App does not collect personal information, we generally hold nothing about you. You remain in control of the data on your device: you can withdraw the Photos permission or delete the App at any time.

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for targeted advertising. For correspondence you have sent us, you can ask us for a copy or to delete it by emailing tastegames13@gmail.com. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the App or our practices. Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

For questions about this policy or Limpid, contact Taste Games at tastegames13@gmail.com.
