Sunhue Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Taste Games (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in Sunhue (the “App”), a UV index app for iPhone. If you have questions, contact us at tastegames13@gmail.com.

The short version

Sunhue has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking, and we do not operate any server that receives data from the App. To show the UV index, the App asks a weather service for the forecast of an approximate place. That is the only thing that leaves your device.

Location

If you allow it, the App reads your approximate location while it is open. It never asks for precise or background location. Before any request the position is rounded to two decimal places, about one kilometre. You can refuse location altogether and choose a city instead, and you can withdraw the permission at any time in your device’s settings.

The weather service

The UV forecast comes from Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com). The App sends it the rounded position of each place you keep, and, when you search for a city, the letters you type. The App’s widgets ask for the same forecast for the place the App stored. These requests go directly from your device to Open-Meteo; as with any internet request, the service sees your IP address. We do not receive these requests, and we do not combine them with anything else. Open-Meteo’s own privacy policy applies to its service.

What stays on your device

Your places (the rounded position and the name), your skin type, your settings, the sunscreen timer and the last forecast are stored on your device, in the storage the App shares with its own widgets. They are not uploaded and not sent to us.

Notifications

The sunscreen reminder, the morning summary and the UV alert are notifications that the App schedules on your device; no server is involved. You can turn them off in the App or in your device’s settings.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information through the App. Sunhue does not ask for your name, email address, or any other contact details, and it does not use advertising identifiers.

Purchases

Sunhue is free. It has no purchases, no subscriptions and no advertising.

Not medical advice

The time to burn shown in the App is an estimate based on the skin type you choose. It is not medical advice.

Information Apple may share with us

If you have chosen on your device to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide us with aggregated statistics and crash reports about the App. This information does not identify you.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use this only to reply to you and to resolve your request, we keep it only for as long as that takes or as the law requires, and we do not sell or share it.

Children’s privacy

Sunhue is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children or from anyone else through the App. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, please contact us so that we can delete it.

Data retention and security

Because the App keeps its data on your device, that data is protected by the security of your device and stays there until you remove a place in the App or delete the App. We hold no copy of it.

International data processing

Requests to the weather service are handled by Open-Meteo’s servers, which may be in a country other than the one where you live. Email you send us is handled by our email provider and may be processed in other countries as well.

Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, including the General Data Protection Regulation in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, and privacy laws of states in the United States such as California, you may have rights of access, correction, deletion and objection regarding personal information. Because the App’s data stays on your device, you exercise most of these rights there.

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for targeted advertising. For correspondence you have sent us, you can ask us for a copy or to delete it by emailing tastegames13@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the App or our practices. Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

For questions about this policy or Sunhue, contact Taste Games at tastegames13@gmail.com.
