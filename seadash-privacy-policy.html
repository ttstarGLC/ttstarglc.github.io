Seadash Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Taste Games (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in Seadash (the “App”), a jet-ski racing game for iPhone. We keep it simple: the game is played on your device and we do not collect personal information.

The short version

Seadash has no accounts, no advertising, no third-party analytics and no servers of ours. Your progress is saved on your iPhone. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple.

Your game progress

Coins, gems, levels, stars, the boats you own and your settings are stored in a file on your device. Deleting the App deletes them. We hold no copy and cannot restore it.

In-app purchases

Seadash offers optional in-app purchases (gem packs and premium boats). Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store; we never see your name, payment details or Apple Account. The App receives only a confirmation from Apple that a purchase was made, and keeps a record of it on your device so the same purchase is not counted twice. Boats you buy can be restored on any device signed in to the same Apple Account with “Restore purchases” in the game.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information through the App. Seadash does not ask for your name, email address or any other contact details, it does not use advertising or tracking technologies, and it does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Information Apple may share with us

If you have chosen on your device to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide us with aggregated statistics and crash reports about the App. This information is anonymised by Apple.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use this only to reply to you and to resolve your request, we keep it only for as long as that takes, and we do not share it.

Children’s privacy

Seadash is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or from anyone else through the App. Purchases go through Apple, where parents can require approval with Ask to Buy and Screen Time.

Data retention and security

Because the App keeps its data on your device, that data is protected by the security of your device and stays there until you delete the App.

Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, including the General Data Protection Regulation in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, and privacy laws of states in the United States, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of personal information about you. As the App holds its data only on your device, deleting the App removes everything it kept. We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for targeted advertising. For correspondence you have sent us, you can ask us for a copy or to delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the App or our practices. Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

For questions about this policy or Seadash, contact Taste Games at tastegames13@gmail.com.
