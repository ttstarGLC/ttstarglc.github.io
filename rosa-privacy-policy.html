Rosa Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Taste Games (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in Rosa (the “App”), a private cycle tracker for iPhone and iPad. If you have questions, contact us at tastegames13@gmail.com.

The short version

Rosa works entirely on your device. What you log about your cycle is encrypted on your phone and never leaves it. The App has no accounts, no advertising, no third-party analytics and no tracking, it makes no network connections of its own, and we do not operate any server that receives data from the App. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal information, and we cannot see anything you enter.

What you log

The days of your period and their flow, your symptoms and moods, your notes, and, if you choose to track them, pill and intimacy entries, together with your settings, are kept in a database on your device. The database is encrypted with a key that is created on your device and kept in its secure storage. Nothing in it is uploaded, sent to us, or shared with anyone. Your predictions are calculated on your device from what you have logged.

You can delete a single entry at any time, and “Delete everything” in the App’s settings removes the database, its key and every scheduled reminder. All of it is also removed when you delete the App.

Device backups

By default Rosa keeps its database out of iCloud and computer backups of your device. If you turn on “Include in device backup”, the encrypted database and its key become part of the backups you make of your device, which are handled by Apple under your Apple Account, as described in the Apple Privacy Policy. We have no access to them.

The lock, reminders and the report

If you turn on the lock, your device checks Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode; the App only learns whether the check succeeded and never receives your biometric data. Reminders are notifications that the App schedules on your device; no server is involved, and by default their text does not say what they are about. The PDF report for your doctor is made on your device when you ask for it, leaves your notes out, goes only where you choose to send it, and is deleted from the App afterwards.

Not a medical device

Rosa is not a contraceptive and not a medical device. Its predictions are estimates from your own entries.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information through the App, and in particular we do not collect health information. Rosa does not ask for your name, email address, or any other contact details, and it does not use advertising identifiers.

Purchases

Rosa Plus is a one-time purchase processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details, billing address, or Apple Account information, and nothing about your cycle is part of a purchase. The App checks on your device, through Apple’s systems, whether the purchase has been made. Apple’s handling of your information is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

Information Apple may share with us

If you have chosen on your device to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide us with aggregated statistics and crash reports about the App. This information does not identify you and contains nothing you entered in the App.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use this only to reply to you and to resolve your request, we keep it only for as long as that takes or as the law requires, and we do not sell or share it.

Children’s privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or from anyone else through the App. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, please contact us so that we can delete it.

Data retention and security

Because the App keeps its data on your device, that data stays there until you delete it or the App. We hold no copy of it.

International data processing

The App does not send your data anywhere, so it is not transferred to other countries by us. Email you send us is handled by our email provider and may be processed in countries other than the one where you live.

Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, including the General Data Protection Regulation in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, and privacy laws of states in the United States such as California, you may have rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, and portability, and the right not to be discriminated against for using them. Since the App does not collect personal information, we generally hold nothing about you. You remain in control of the data on your device: you can edit or delete what you have logged, export a report, use “Delete everything”, or delete the App at any time.

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for targeted advertising. For correspondence you have sent us, you can ask us for a copy or to delete it by emailing tastegames13@gmail.com. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the App or our practices. Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

For questions about this policy or Rosa, contact Taste Games at tastegames13@gmail.com.
