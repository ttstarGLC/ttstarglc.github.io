Voyager 3 Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Taste Games (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in Voyager 3 (the “Game”). If you have questions, contact us at tastegames13@gmail.com.

Accounts and information we request

Voyager 3 does not offer accounts or login. Taste Games does not directly ask for names, passwords, account profiles, payment information, or precise contact information through the Game.

Advertising and information processed by providers

The Game uses advertising services provided through Unity LevelPlay, ironSource Exchange, Unity Ads, and Google AdMob. These providers may process information such as device identifiers, IP addresses, approximate location inferred from an IP address, advertising interactions, diagnostic information, device information, and consent choices where applicable.

This processing may support ad delivery, frequency limiting, fraud prevention, reporting, measurement, and personalized advertising only where legally permitted and consented to. The Game may show interstitial advertisements and optional rewarded advertisements. Watching a rewarded advertisement is voluntary.

These providers operate under their own terms and privacy policies. Please review the Google Privacy Policy, Unity Privacy Policy, and ironSource / LevelPlay Privacy Policy.

Consent for the EEA, United Kingdom, and Switzerland

Where required, advertising consent choices are requested for users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland. Eligible users can review or change their advertising consent choices using the Privacy Options button inside the Game. Personalized advertising is used only when permitted by applicable law and, where required, with consent.

United States state privacy disclosures

Depending on your state and the information processed, you may have rights to know, access, correct, delete, or opt out of certain processing, including targeted advertising or the sale or sharing of personal information as those terms are defined by applicable law. To exercise a request related to information handled by Taste Games, contact us at tastegames13@gmail.com. Advertising choices may also be available through the Privacy Options button and your device settings. Third-party providers may handle requests relating to information they process under their own privacy policies.

Children’s privacy

Voyager 3 is not directed specifically toward children. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to Taste Games through the Game, please contact us so we can review the concern.

Data retention

Taste Games retains information it directly receives only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer period is required or permitted by law. Advertising providers may retain information according to their own policies and practices; Taste Games does not control every third-party retention period.

Data security

We use reasonable measures intended to protect information we directly handle. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

International data processing

Information processed through the Game or its advertising providers may be processed in countries other than the one where you live. Those countries may have data protection laws that differ from those in your jurisdiction.

Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights concerning personal information, including rights of access, deletion, correction, restriction, objection, portability, or withdrawal of consent. To ask about a right related to information directly handled by Taste Games, email tastegames13@gmail.com. You may also use the in-game Privacy Options button, where available, to manage advertising consent choices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the Game or our practices. Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

For questions about this policy or Voyager 3, contact Taste Games at tastegames13@gmail.com.
