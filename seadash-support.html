Seadash Support

Need a hand with Seadash? Email us at tastegames13@gmail.com and we’ll get back to you, usually within a couple of days.

I bought a boat and it’s gone

Open Settings (the gear on the home screen) and tap Restore next to Purchases, or the Restore purchases button at the bottom of Shop → Gems. Make sure you’re signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

I bought gems but didn’t get them

Gems are added as soon as Apple confirms the payment. If the game was closed in the middle, open it again and they will arrive. If they still don’t, email us with the date of the purchase.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

The game feels slow or my phone gets warm

Seadash adjusts its graphics on its own to stay smooth. You can also pick a lighter setting in Settings → Graphics.
