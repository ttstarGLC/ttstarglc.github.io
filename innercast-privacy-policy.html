Innercast Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Taste Games (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in Innercast (the “App”), an iPhone app that forecasts how your day is likely to go from what came before it. We are committed to protecting your privacy and to being clear about what the App does.

The short version

Innercast works entirely on your iPhone. It reads a few things from the Health app with your permission, keeps what it learns in a file on your device, and sends nothing anywhere. The App has no accounts, no advertising, no third-party analytics and no servers of ours.

Health data

With your permission, the App reads from Apple Health: sleep analysis (how long you slept and when you fell asleep), step counts, menstrual flow entries (to know the day of your cycle), heart rate variability and sleeping wrist temperature (the last two only exist when an Apple Watch or another device writes them). The App only reads this data; it never writes to Health. It uses the data on your device to compute the morning forecast and its reasons, and to learn, from your evening answers, what tends to come before your harder days.

Health data is never sent off your device, never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with anyone. You can withdraw the App’s access at any time in the Health app under Sharing → Apps, or in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health; the App then works without the signals it can no longer read.

Your answers and the forecast

Your morning guess, your evening answer (how the day went, and any tags you choose), the daily forecast and the weights the App has learnt are stored in a file on your device. “Start over” in the Model tab deletes all of it; deleting the App does too. We hold no copy.

Reminders

Reminders are notifications the App schedules on your device at the times you choose; no server is involved.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information through the App. Innercast does not ask for your name, email address, or any other contact details, it does not use advertising or tracking technologies, and it does not include third-party analytics.

Information Apple may share with us

If you have chosen on your device to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide us with aggregated statistics and crash reports about the App. This information is anonymised by Apple and does not include your Health data or your answers.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use this only to reply to you and to resolve your request, we keep it only for as long as that takes, and we do not share it.

Children’s privacy

Innercast is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children or from anyone else through the App.

Data retention and security

Because the App keeps its data on your device, that data is protected by the security of your device and stays there until you delete it or the App. We hold no copy of it and cannot restore it.

International data processing

The App does not send your data anywhere, so it is not transferred to other countries by us. Email you send us is handled by our email provider and may be processed where that provider operates.

Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, including the General Data Protection Regulation in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, and privacy laws of states in the United States, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of personal information about you. As the App holds its data only on your device, you exercise these rights there: the Health app controls what the App may read, and “Start over” or deleting the App removes everything it kept.

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for targeted advertising. For correspondence you have sent us, you can ask us for a copy or to delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the App or our practices. Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
