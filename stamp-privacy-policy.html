Stamp Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Taste Games (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in Stamp (the “App”), a map of the countries you have visited for iPhone and iPad. If you have questions, contact us at tastegames13@gmail.com.

The short version

Stamp works entirely on your device. The countries you mark, your dates, notes, cities and photos never leave it. The App has no accounts, no advertising, no third-party analytics, no tracking and no purchases, it makes no network connections, and we do not operate any server that receives data from the App. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal information.

Your travels

The countries you mark as visited, lived in or wanted, the dates of your visits, your notes, the cities you add and the country you call home are kept in a file on your device. They are not uploaded, not sent to us, and not shared with anyone. This data is removed when you delete the App.

Photos

You can add photos to a country. You choose them in the system photo picker, so the App sees only the pictures you pick and never reads your library. Copies are stored on your device with the rest of the App’s data and are not uploaded.

The widget, the poster and backups

The Home Screen widget shows your map and your count; it reads them on your device, in the storage the App shares with its own widget. The share poster is an image made on your device; it goes only where you choose to send it. A backup is a file written when you ask for one and read back when you ask; it too goes only where you put it.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information through the App. Stamp does not ask for your name, email address, or any other contact details, it does not use your location, and it does not use advertising identifiers.

Information Apple may share with us

If you have chosen on your device to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide us with aggregated statistics and crash reports about the App. This information does not identify you and contains nothing you entered in the App.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use this only to reply to you and to resolve your request, we keep it only for as long as that takes or as the law requires, and we do not sell or share it.

Children’s privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or from anyone else through the App. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, please contact us so that we can delete it.

Data retention and security

Because the App keeps its data on your device, that data stays there until you delete it or the App. We hold no copy of it.

International data processing

The App does not send your data anywhere, so it is not transferred to other countries by us. Email you send us is handled by our email provider and may be processed in countries other than the one where you live.

Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, including the General Data Protection Regulation in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, and privacy laws of states in the United States such as California, you may have rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, and portability, and the right not to be discriminated against for using them. Since the App does not collect personal information, we generally hold nothing about you. You remain in control of the data on your device: you can remove a mark, a note or a photo, write a backup, or delete the App at any time.

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for targeted advertising. For correspondence you have sent us, you can ask us for a copy or to delete it by emailing tastegames13@gmail.com. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the App or our practices. Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

For questions about this policy or Stamp, contact Taste Games at tastegames13@gmail.com.
